## Find your way around

| Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Start a session, or jump back into a recent one under **Jump back in** |
| **Search** | Find any session, track or playlist |
| **Sessions** | Your sessions, under **Active now**, **Pinned** and **History** |
| **Library** | Your tracks, under **Playlists**, **Favorites**, **Saved** and **History** |
| **Lounge** | Playlists other people on your instance share with you |

Tap your avatar for **Profile**, **Your Stats**, **Settings**, **Mode** and **Sign out**. The full player opens when you start music or tap the mini player.

## Ask Roadie

Roadie is the AI assistant inside the app. Ask it how something in Liedmotif works, such as "How do I listen offline?", and it answers from these docs and says where in the app to look. Roadie can also play your music and control the player, do a few things in your library for you, change a few of your own settings, and prepare anything that costs for you to confirm: a new session, a Surprise Me, a change of vibe, more like a track, New music on, or a new cover. It never removes anything.

- **Open it:** tap the round button with the radio and sparkles, **Ask Roadie**, at the bottom right. On a wide screen Roadie opens as a sidebar beside the page, so you can keep using the page while you ask, and the same button closes it. On a phone Roadie fills the screen, and the close button in its top bar brings you back. On a phone the full player has the button in its top bar instead.
- **Ask:** type a question and press Enter, or pick one of the suggested questions. The answer appears as it's written, and the stop button ends it early.
- **Ask about your own music:** "What is playing?" gets the session or playlist, the track and, for a session, whether New music is on, off or finished. "How many tracks do I have left?" or "How many covers can I still make?" gets the tracks and covers left today and this month: for a member their own budget and the shared one, for the admin the instance limits.
- **Find your music:** Roadie finds your sessions, playlists and tracks the way [Search](#search) does: by title, genre or mood, and tracks also by their lyrics. "Which song has the line about the lighthouse?" gets the track and the matching line, and "What was that rainy session last week?" gets the session and when you last played it. When nothing matches, Roadie also sees the names spelled most like what you asked for, so a typo or "und" for "&" still finds it, or Roadie asks which one you meant. Without anything to search for, Roadie lists the most recent ones, and it can list the tracks of one session or playlist. Roadie finds what you can find in the app yourself, and it names up to 10 results, or the first 30 tracks of a list.
- **Let Roadie do it:** Roadie can add a track to your Favorites, add up to 20 tracks to one of your playlists, make a new playlist, rename or pin one of your playlists, rename or pin one of your sessions, rename one of your tracks, and switch New music off for your running session. "Favorite this", "put this in my running playlist" and "rename this to Harbor Lights" mean the track that is playing, and "call this session Sunday Coffee" or "pin this session" the session that is playing; with nothing playing, Roadie asks which one. A name follows the same rules as **Rename** in the app, and downloaded files get the new name too. Tracks shared with you in the Lounge aren't yours to rename. Pinning past 10 sessions gets the app's own answer: unpin one yourself first. Each thing Roadie did shows as a line in the chat, such as "Added to Favorites: Night Drive", with **Open** to go to where it now is. The app writes that line itself, so it shows what really happened. The pages and the player update right away. Roadie does up to 3 things per message; ask again for more.
- **Change a setting:** Roadie changes four of your own settings when you ask: **Include the owner in Covers** (Always, Regularly, Seldom or Never), **Blend consecutive Tracks** (on or off), **Crossfade length** (2 to 12 seconds) and **Default language**. Say "show me on covers only seldom" or "set the crossfade to 8 seconds", and it is saved right away, with no tap. The line in the chat shows the old and the new choice, such as "Crossfade length: 6 s → 8 s", with **Open** to go to its settings page. A change to blending also reaches the music that is playing, from the next transition; a new crossfade length may wait one more transition, when the next one is already planned. **Include the owner in Covers** needs a reference photo on your own card in **Settings › People** first, as on that page. A change counts as one of the 3 things per message.
- **Play your music:** say "play my running playlist", "resume the focus session from yesterday" or "play Night Drive", and Roadie starts it in the player right away, with no tap. A session resumes where you stopped it, as **Resume** does. It keeps its New music setting, so a session with New music on can generate new tracks from your budget again. A playlist plays from the top, or from the track you name, and playlists shared with you in the Lounge play too. A single track plays through the session it came from, as it does when you tap it in the library. If that session is deleted, Roadie can only play the track inside a playlist that holds it. The line in the chat says what plays, such as "Playing: Running · 24 Tracks". If a new session is still starting, the app asks first, as it does for any other music: **Play this instead** or **Keep starting**. Roadie plays only music you already have: it creates no session and changes no setting this way. Playing counts as one of the 2 player commands per message.
- **Control the player:** say "pause", "stop", "continue", "next", "go back", "play that again" or "loop this", and Roadie does it in the player right away, with no tap. It works while a session or a playlist is in the player, from any page, and the player and the mini player follow as if you had pressed the button yourself. With nothing in the player yet, "continue" plays your running session. "Next" is the app's **Skip**, and "loop this" switches **Loop Track** on. "Stop" pauses the music, the same as "pause", for a session and for a playlist. To end a session, as **Stop Session** does, say "end the session" or "close the session". Each command shows as a short line in the chat, such as "Paused". Roadie takes up to 2 player commands per message.
- **Set the sleep timer:** "stop the music in 30 minutes" sets the [sleep timer](#sleep-timer) to any whole number of minutes from 5 to 90, and replaces a running one. "How long is left on the timer?" and "cancel the timer" work too. It's the same timer as in the options menu, which shows it as well.
- **If the music doesn't start:** sending your message lets the browser start the audio, so "continue" or "play my running playlist" just plays. If your browser still blocks it, the line in the chat shows **Tap to play**: one tap starts the music.
- **Start a session from the chat:** ask Roadie for new music, such as "Start something calm for reading". Roadie prepares the session and shows a card in the chat: your request in your words, **Instrumental** or **With vocals**, the length or **No limit**, and how many tracks you have left. Say how long it should run, such as "for 45 minutes" or "with no limit"; if you say neither, Roadie asks before it shows a card, so a session never keeps generating without a limit unless you said so. Roadie skips the question when **Generate new Tracks by default** is off in **Settings › Generation**: such a session gets its first batch and then repeats. It can't start with no limit then: name a length, or switch **New music** on once it plays. Nothing starts and nothing is bought until you press **Start Session** on the card. Pressing it starts the session as **Start Session** on **Home** does: the player opens, and on a phone Roadie closes to show it. Roadie asks for instrumental music unless you ask for vocals. A card works once, and Roadie shows one card per message. If your budget has too little left, Roadie says so and shows no card. If the start is refused after you press the button, the player shows why and the card says **This Session did not start**.
- **Surprise Me from the chat:** say "surprise me", and Roadie prepares a suggestion from your listening the way [Surprise Me](#surprise-me) does. Add what you want: "something more adventurous", a situation such as "for the morning" or "for focus", "with vocals" or "instrumental", and a length such as "for an hour" or "with no limit". The card shows the suggestion's name, genres, mood, energy and vocals, its length (the one Surprise Me picked, unless you named one) and how many tracks you have left. Preparing it uses your writing AI a little and buys no music. Ask again for another one: it replaces the last, and **Home** shows the same suggestion, so there is only ever one. Nothing starts until you press **Start Session** on the card, which starts it as Surprise Me's own button does and opens the player. The language stays **Auto** and your profile is used; for anything else, use Surprise Me on **Home**. If your budget has too little left, Roadie says so and shows no card. If the suggestion was replaced or dismissed elsewhere, or the start is refused after you press the button, the card says why. A suggestion you don't start stays on **Home** until you dismiss it there.
- **Change the vibe, more like this, New music on:** the three other things that generate music get the same card. "Make it slower, with a cello" prepares a **Change Vibe** for your running session, with your words on the card. "More like this" prepares **More Like This**: a new session in the style of the track that is playing, or of up to 10 tracks you name, including tracks shared with you in the Lounge. "Turn New music back on for 90 minutes" or "with no limit" prepares what the player's **New music** control offers, **Up to a duration** or **No limit**; if you say neither, Roadie asks. Nothing changes and nothing is bought until you press the button on the card: **Change Vibe**, **Start new Session** or **Save**. The button does what the app's own control does: More Like This opens the player with the new session, and the other two change the running session where you are. Roadie shows no card, and says why, when your budget has nothing left, when there is no running session, when the session has reached its duration target, or when it already holds the length you asked for. If the app refuses after you press the button, the card says so; for a change of vibe the player shows why.
- **A new cover from the chat:** say "make a new cover for this, darker, neon city", and Roadie prepares one new cover for the session or playlist that is playing, or for one of yours you name. Your words become the art direction, up to 500 characters; say none and Roadie just makes another. The card shows what the cover is for, your art direction and the cover budget line the cover dialog shows, such as "Covers today: 3 of 10". The style stays the session's own (**Auto** for a playlist), **Enhance prompts** follows your setting, and People reference photos aren't used; for those, use the cover dialog. Nothing is generated until you press **Generate cover**, which uses one cover from your budget, like **Reroll Cover Art**. The new cover then lands in **Previous covers** as a preview: your cover changes only when you pick it there. Roadie shows no card, and says why, when covers aren't set up or are switched off, when your cover budget is used up, when the session or playlist already keeps 10 covers, or when a cover for it is already being generated. Playlists shared with you in the Lounge aren't yours to give a cover. If the app refuses after you press the button, the card says why.
- **What Roadie doesn't do:** Roadie only adds, renames and pins. It can't unfavorite or unpin, take a track out of a playlist, unsave or delete anything, and it can't dislike a track, change the volume or jump to a position in a track. Ask for one of those and Roadie points you to the page where you do it yourself. It changes only the four settings named above: every other setting is yours to change on its page, and so is anything that can spend more, such as a limit or **Tracks generated in advance**. Roadie plays and controls music you already have; anything that generates new music or a cover it only prepares, for you to confirm on the card. Picking an earlier cover, uploading one or deleting one stays in the cover dialog.
- **Change how Roadie talks:** Roadie is laid-back and a little snippy by default. Under **Settings › Roadie** you can give it a tone of your own. Write up to 600 characters in **Tone**, or tap **Happy**, **Polite** or **Depressed** to fill the field and edit from there. **Roadie's own** empties the field, which brings the default tone back. Press **Save tone**, and it applies from your next message. The tone is yours alone, and it changes the wording only: what Roadie can do stays the same, and it still shows a card before anything that costs. The page is there for everyone who has Roadie.
- **Follow the answer:** an answer can end in a button that opens the right page in the app. Links to these docs open the website.
- **Start over:** **New chat** clears the conversation. The conversation stays while you move between pages and is gone when you reload.

Roadie answers in the language you write in. Names of screens and buttons stay as the app shows them.

**For members:** the admin can give each member a daily message allowance. When yours runs low, Roadie says how many messages are left today, and once it's used up, when you can ask again. The admin can also switch Roadie off for a member, and then the button isn't shown. Both are set under **Settings › Members**, see [members](/docs/members).

**Good to know:** Roadie runs on your writing AI, so it only appears when one is connected, and each question is a small request to that provider. If the writing AI fails or you ask a lot in a short time, Roadie says so, and your question stays in the field so you can send it again. What Roadie sends where is in [privacy and security](/docs/privacy-and-security#the-writing-ai).

## Ask for music

### Start a session

On **Home**, describe the music in your own words, up to 2,000 characters, and press **Start Session**. An activity, a mood, a place or a sound all work.

The player opens right away, and anything already playing carries on until the new session is ready. While the request is being prepared, **Cancel** stops it. You have one running session at a time, so a new one stops the old one, which you can resume later.

The first track can take a while. The player tells you what's happening, from "Connecting to provider…" to "Almost there — preparing your first track…". If you pick other music while a session is starting, Liedmotif asks first: **Play this instead** cancels the start, and **Keep starting** doesn't.

If your budget has too little left, no session starts and nothing is bought. A dialog offers **Resume a Session** and **Browse Library**, plus a link to your limits. See [costs and limits](/docs/costs-and-limits).

### Example requests

While the field is empty, it cycles through worked examples of good requests. They're only examples: nothing is sent until you type your own.

### With or without a writing AI

A writing AI is optional. It's a text service that turns your words into one steady direction for the whole session, and gives sessions and tracks creative names. `.env` calls it a text LLM. Without one, your words reach the music model exactly as written, and your fine-tune picks apply either way. The admin connects one in **Settings › AI Providers**, and [providers and keys](/docs/providers-and-keys#writing-ai) compares the two in full.

Without one, **Home** says **No Writing AI connected**. If a connected writing AI fails, the session doesn't quietly start without it: you get an error and can try again.

### Fine-tune picks

Open **Fine-tune** under the request field. There's no need to repeat these picks in your words.

| Pick | Choices | What to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Session duration** | **Auto**, or up to 180 minutes in steps of 5 | See [Session duration](#session-duration) |
| **Genre** | Up to three | They blend, and the first one leads. **For you** marks suggestions from your listening, and **+ All genres** opens the full list |
| **Energy** | **Auto**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High** | |
| **Vocals** | **Auto**, **Instrumental**, **Vocals** | See [Instrumental or vocals](#instrumental-or-vocals) |
| **Voice** | **Any**, **Female**, **Male** | Shows only when you pick **Vocals** |
| **Language** | **Auto Detect**, or one of 23 languages | Guides track titles and vocals, including the titles of instrumental sessions |

**Language** starts on your **Default language** from **Settings › Generation**.

### Session duration

Set a target for how much new music a session makes. When it reaches the target, it stops generating and replays its tracks at no cost.

- The target counts generated music, not listening time. Played and skipped tracks count, and disliked or deleted ones don't.
- It can overshoot by one batch, because the provider makes tracks in pairs.
- The music keeps playing after the target. To stop it at a set time, use the [sleep timer](#sleep-timer).
- On **Auto**, a writing AI picks up a duration you asked for in words. Without one, the session keeps going. A slider setting always wins over your words.

While a target is set, the player's [New music](#new-music) status shows progress such as "12 / 40 min generated", then "New music finished · 42 min" when it's reached. Tap it to set, change or clear the target. You can also do that in **Change Vibe › Session Details**. Setting a target turns on new music for that session.

### Instrumental or vocals

Sessions are instrumental by default. With **Vocals** on **Auto**, a writing AI still chooses instrumental unless your words ask for singing. Without a writing AI, **Auto** means instrumental. Pick **Vocals** to be sure. The choice is per session, and there's no global setting for it.

### Fresh lyrics

With a writing AI, each new pair of vocal tracks gets its own lyric angle within the session's direction, so songs don't keep singing the same scene. A reroll reuses the angle of the track it replaces. If that step fails, the tracks use the session's general lyric notes and still generate.

### Guardrails

Open **Guardrails** under the request field to set limits for one session.

- **Follow style** goes from **Loose** to **Strict** and starts at 0.65. It sets how strictly the music sticks to the style.
- **Surprise** goes from **Safe** to **Wild** and starts at 0.30. It sets how experimental the music may get.
- **Avoid** takes up to 500 characters of sounds you don't want.

Every track of the vibe follows them, including rerolls. You can change them later with [Change Vibe](#change-the-vibe), which counts as a new vibe and buys its first tracks. A session started with More Like This inherits them.

### Surprise Me

Press the dice beside the request field. Pick a **Theme**, a **Language**, **Vocals** and **Explore** (**Familiar** or **Adventurous**), then press **Find a surprise**. You get a proposed session shaped by your listening.

From the preview, **Start Session** starts it, **Try another** proposes a new one, **Change settings** goes back and **Dismiss** throws it away. A proposal buys no music, though it does use your writing AI if one is connected. It also works without one. Like starting a session, it needs budget left.

The proposal fills in the form on **Home**, so you can edit it first. An unstarted proposal is still there on your next visit. The theme **A song about me** always has vocals.

Or ask [Roadie](#ask-roadie): "surprise me". The suggestion Roadie prepares is the one Surprise Me shows.

## Listen

### Continuous sessions

A session keeps its direction and makes more tracks while you listen. It only generates while something is playing. Pause for long enough and it stops itself, after 15 minutes by default, and stays ready to resume. Playing a playlist instead also leaves it idle.

### Tracks ready ahead

Liedmotif keeps some tracks ready so there's little or no waiting. Set how many in **Settings › Generation › Tracks generated in advance**, from 1 to 10, with 3 as the default. The admin's limits can lower this. A change reaches a running session within about a minute.

### New music

Each session either makes new tracks or replays what it has. Tap the status at the left of the player's icon row, or **New music** in the options menu, and choose:

- **Off** plays the tracks you have and buys nothing.
- **Up to a duration** makes new music until the session reaches the length you set on the slider, up to 180 minutes.
- **No limit** keeps making new music for as long as you listen.

**Off** applies at once. Anything that could spend waits for **Save**, so a stray tap can't cost you anything. The sheet also shows the minutes generated so far, the tracks you have left today, this month or in total when a limit applies, and "Only generates while you listen."

The status tells you what's actually happening. Phones show the shorter wording where the table lists one.

| Status | On a phone | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Generating 2" | | Two tracks are being bought. With a duration, a second line shows progress such as "22 / 60 min". A ring around the icon fills as each track lands |
| "Finishing 2" | | **New music** is off, and two tracks already paid for are still being made |
| "12 / 60 min generated" | "12 / 60 min" | The session has a duration and isn't buying anything right now. A "+", as in "12+ / 60 min", means some track lengths aren't known yet, and new tracks wait until they are |
| "New music on · no limit" | "On · no limit" | New tracks come whenever the session needs them |
| "New music finished · 62 min" | "Done · 62 min" | The duration is reached, so the session replays its tracks. It can run a little over, because tracks come in pairs |
| "Limit reached · Repeating" | "Limit · Repeat" | A budget ran out, so the session replays its tracks until there's room again |
| "New music paused" | "Paused" | A provider or worker problem is holding it. Ready tracks keep playing |
| "New music off" | | The session plays the tracks it has |

Even with **New music** off, a new session gets its first batch, and a Change Vibe still buys the new vibe's first tracks. Tracks already started finish too. Reaching a budget or a duration also makes a session repeat. Choose how new sessions start in **Settings › Generation › Generate new Tracks by default**.

### The player

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| List | Opens **Tracks in this Session** or **Tracks in this Playlist**. Tap one to play it |
| Back | Restarts the track, or goes to the previous one if you're within its first 3 seconds |
| Play and pause | Starts and stops the music |
| Skip | Moves to the next track |
| Repeat button | Cycles through **Loop Session**, **Loop Track** and **Shuffle Session**, or the same for a playlist |
| Progress bar | Drag to seek. With a keyboard, the arrow keys move 5 seconds |
| Star and plus | **Favorite this Track** and **Add to Playlist** |

Swipe the cover left to skip or right to go back. In a session's track list, a "New vibe from here" line marks where a Change Vibe begins.

**Shuffle** changes the play order on your device only, and the session's order stays as it was. **Loop Track** blends the end of a track into its start when the audio allows, and otherwise restarts it. New generation waits while it's on.

### The options menu

The **…** button in the player holds everything else, in two groups that act on different things.

| Group | While a session plays | While a playlist plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top tiles | **Lyrics**, **Session Cover**, **Blend Tracks**, **Playlist** | **Playlist Cover**, **Blend Tracks**, **Playlist** |
| First rows | **New music**, **Available offline** | **Available offline** |
| **This Track** | **Rename Track**, **Track Data**, **Loop a Part**, **Reroll Track**, **More Like This**, **Download Track**, **Delete Track** | **Lyrics**, **Rename Track**, **Track Data**, **Loop a Part**, **Download Track**, **Remove from this Playlist**, **Delete Track** |
| This session or playlist | **Change Vibe**, **Sleep Timer**, **Rename Session**, **Convert to Playlist**, **Download Session**, **Delete Session** | **Go to Playlist**, **Sleep Timer**, **Rename Playlist**, **Download Playlist**, **More Like This**, **Delete Playlist** |
| Bottom | **Stop Session** | |

Favorites has no rename or delete row. On a playlist shared with you, the rows only its owner may use are gone.

### Loop a part

Repeat the passage you love, without a gap. Turn it on with the **Loop a Part** button in the player's control row, or in the options menu. The progress bar becomes a waveform.

- Drag across the waveform to mark the part. A tap jumps instead.
- Drag either end to adjust it. Dragging the end plays the last 2 seconds so you can judge the join by ear.
- Marks snap to the beat when the track's tempo is known.
- Press the **×** beside the times to stop looping, or turn the tool off.

Looping buys nothing, and generation waits while it runs. The loop ends when you stop the music, jump away from the part, change track or change the vibe. The mini player shows "Looping a part".

A track that's still streaming can't loop until its full audio is ready. Marks don't snap on a playlist or in the lounge. The Offline Library's player doesn't have this tool, and a device reporting under 4 GB of memory can't use it.

### Blending and crossfades

Tracks crossfade at matched loudness, and the fade lines up with the beat when the audio allows.

To switch blending on or off at once, use the blend button in the player or the **Blend Tracks** tile. It applies from the next transition. For the length, open **Settings › Playback**, set **Crossfade length** from 2 to 12 seconds, with 6 as the default, and press **Save playback**. **Blend consecutive Tracks** on the same page does the same as the player's button.

The player picks up saved settings right away. Fades that snap to the beat can run a little shorter or longer than you set.

If your browser can't run the blending engine, tracks simply play one after another. Liedmotif also recovers from common audio interruptions on phones.

### Sleep timer

Open the options menu and pick **Sleep Timer**. Choose 5 to 90 minutes, in steps of 5, and press **Start timer**. Or ask [Roadie](#ask-roadie): "stop the music in 30 minutes". It works for sessions and playlists, including ones in the lounge and the Offline Library.

The music fades out over the last ten seconds, so it's silent at the time you chose, and then pauses. The session stays ready to resume, and nothing new gets made. The timer keeps running while you browse or switch music, and the menu shows the minutes left, with **Restart timer** and **Turn off**. Reloading the page clears it.

### Lyrics

Vocal tracks show a **Lyrics** tab on the cover, which turns the cover over to show the words. The expand button, or **Lyrics** in the options menu, opens them full size. Instrumental tracks have no tab.

The words can appear before the track is ready, under "Writing the words". When timing data is available, the lines scroll along and you can tap one to jump there. The footer tells you where things stand: "Tap a line to jump", "Line timing is on its way" or "No line timing for this Track".

### What the session is doing

The progress bar names what's happening next to the track number, for example "Track 3/7 · Generating 2". It can also say "Changing vibe…", "Queuing new vibe…", "New vibe queued", "Next Session" or "Repeat". It shows nothing when the session is idle.

Real problems get a banner above the cover:

| Banner | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Session Repeat** | A limit was reached, so the session repeats its own tracks |
| **Generation paused** | The music provider is out of credits. Ready tracks keep playing until the admin tops it up |
| **Generation backing off** | The provider is busy. Generation resumes by itself within a few minutes |
| **Catching up** | Generation is behind. Ready tracks keep playing |
| **Generation error** | Generation hit a problem. Ready tracks keep playing while it recovers |

If a session is blocked before its first track and nothing will fix that by itself, the banner offers **Delete Session**.

### The mini player

Music keeps playing while you browse, with a small player in the bottom bar on a phone or the sidebar on a computer. It has the basic controls and opens the full player.

### Play a track from a list

Tapping a track in **Library** or **Search** plays it inside the session it came from. If that session is stopped, it resumes at that track and replaces whatever session is running. If it's set to generate new tracks, it carries on generating. **Most played** in your stats works the same way.

A track whose session was deleted plays as a preview instead. It shows in the mini player and stops when you leave the page. A preview starts no session and can't generate anything.

### Lock-screen and headphone controls

On devices that support them, the track's title, artwork and position show on the lock screen, with play, pause, next, previous and seek. Hardware buttons work too. They need HTTPS or `localhost`. See [phone access](/docs/install/phone-access).

### Available offline

Keep a playlist or session on one device to play it without a connection. Turn on **Available offline**, near the top of its options menu. It works for your own playlists, Favorites, playlists shared with you in the lounge and your own sessions.

The row shows progress such as "Downloading 12 / 30", then "Ready offline". While you're online, the device stays in sync: new tracks arrive and removed ones go.

- The mark is per device, so marking something on your phone does nothing on your laptop.
- Only tracks with finished audio can be kept, so a track that's still generating waits.
- A session's copy holds the tracks made so far. Sessions only generate while playing, so a long session won't fill up ahead of a trip by itself.
- A shared playlist's copy stops playing 7 days after this device last synced. Going online refreshes it.
- It needs HTTPS or `localhost`. Over plain HTTP the switch isn't there, and **Settings › Storage** says why.
- On an iPhone or iPad, add Liedmotif to the home screen, or iOS may clear offline music after a few days.

Offline music stays inside the app. To get a file, use [downloads](#downloads).

### The Offline Library

With no connection, Liedmotif opens the **Offline Library**, a list of what this device holds. It plays in the normal player, with cover, lyrics, track data, skip, seek, crossfades, the sleep timer and lock-screen controls.

Offline, the app is for listening only. Nothing new gets made, saved or edited, and listening here doesn't count in your stats. If the app is already open when the connection drops, controls that need the server go grey and the app shows **Offline**.

### Offline storage on this device

Open **Settings › Storage › On this device** to see what the device holds and how much space it takes. **Remove** drops one collection, and **Clear all** drops everything.

A device holds up to 5 GB by default, and you can set the **Cap** to 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or 50 GB. If something won't fit, it's refused before anything downloads, and nothing is removed to make room. You also can't set the cap below what the device already holds. A track in two collections is stored once. Signing out removes this account's offline music.

## Steer

### Change the vibe

Change direction inside the running session, without starting over. Press **Change Vibe** under the player controls, or use the options menu.

- **New direction** takes your words, such as "Warmer, slower, more acoustic".
- **Session Details** holds the duration, genres, energy, vocals, voice and language, set to the current vibe.
- **Guardrails** holds **Follow style**, **Surprise** and **Avoid**.

Only what you ask for moves. The session keeps its name, cover and tracks.

By default, the tracks already queued play out first. Turn on **Switch to the new vibe as soon as it's ready** to move on sooner: the current track finishes and upcoming old-vibe tracks are set aside, though they stay in the session. This switch isn't available during a track or part loop.

A Change Vibe buys the new vibe's first tracks, even with **New music** off, and it ends any loop. It's refused if your budget has no room for more tracks. It's also refused once the session has reached its duration target, unless the same change raises or clears the target. Changing only the duration isn't a new vibe, and it saves with **Save duration**.

### Dislike a track

Press **Reroll Track** under the player controls, or in the options menu. The track goes at once, and its audio is deleted after about 24 hours. It also leaves any playlist it was in.

You get another track in the same vibe, using the unplayed other half of its batch when there is one, which costs nothing. Otherwise a session that's generating new tracks buys a new one. With **New music** off, or for a track from an earlier vibe, nothing new is bought. A dislike never changes the direction.

### More like this

Start a new session from music you love. The old session stops and stays resumable. There are three ways in:

- **The player:** press **More Like This** and then **Start new Session**. The current track is the seed.
- **A track menu:** **More Like This** in **Library**, **Search** or a playlist's track menu.
- **A playing playlist:** **More Like This** under **This Playlist**, which starts from the playlist's first 10 tracks.

The new session inherits the seed's guardrails, and your profile details apply again even if the seed ran without them. Like any new session, it needs budget left. On a playlist shared with you, it runs on your own budget.

### Skip

Skip moves to the next track and counts as a light negative signal. The track stays in the session, nothing new is bought and the direction doesn't change. In a playlist, skipping records nothing. There's no like button: listening on is the approval.

### Stop and resume

**Stop Session** at the bottom of the options menu stops the music and the generating. Stopped sessions keep their direction, tracks and history with no time limit.

To continue, open the **⋮** menu on a session in **Sessions** and pick **Resume Session**. Tapping a session's card only opens its tracks, so it spends nothing. Resuming isn't blocked by budget limits, because the session can fall back on the music it has.

On a phone, the **History** list shows your last 10 stopped sessions. Use the search box for older ones.

### Pin a session

Pick **Pin Session** in a session's options to keep it in **Pinned** at the top of **Sessions**, up to 10. A pinned session that's running shows under **Active now**. Pinning changes nothing about how it plays. Or ask [Roadie](#ask-roadie): "pin this session".

## Keep and organize

### Your library

**Library** has four tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Playlists** | The playlists you made |
| **Favorites** | Your starred tracks, in the order you set |
| **Saved** | Every track in any of your playlists, Favorites included. These stay when you delete their session |
| **History** | Every track ever generated, marked **Never played** or **Audio unavailable** where that's true |

**Saved** and **History** fill themselves, so you can't edit them by hand. Each tab has its own search box. Tracks don't expire: an unsaved track stays until you delete it or its session.

### Favorites

Tap the star in the player, or pick **Add to Favorites** in a track's menu. Favorites is a built-in playlist. You can reorder it, change its cover and keep it available offline, but you can't rename, delete or share it.

### Playlists

Press **New playlist** in the **Playlists** tab, or file a track from the player with the plus button. **Save to Playlists** shows a checklist, so one track can join several playlists at once. It can also create a new one. Unchecking everything turns the button into **Remove from Library**. A playlist that's shared shows **In the Lounge**, because anything you add is heard there at once.

A playlist's own menu has **Add Tracks**, **Rename Playlist**, **Reorder Tracks**, the cover, **Download all Playlist Tracks** and **Delete Playlist**. Each track's menu has **Remove from Playlist**, **Add to Playlist**, **More Like This**, **Track Data**, **Download Track** and **Delete Track**.

- **Play:** press the round play button, or tap a track to start from there. Playlists loop by default. A playlist never generates music.
- **Reorder:** drag the tracks, then press **Done**. **Cancel** changes nothing. It works for up to 500 tracks.
- **Pin:** pick **Pin Playlist** in a card's menu to list it first in the **Playlists** tab.
- **Names:** up to 60 characters. A track appears once per playlist.

### Add many tracks at once

There are two ways, and each takes up to 100 tracks at a time. In a playlist, **Add Tracks** lists your full history: tick the tracks and press **Add**. Tracks already in it are locked. Or in **Saved** or **History**, press **Select**, tap the tracks, press the **Add to playlist** button that shows your count and choose a playlist. For more than 100, repeat in batches.

### Turn a session into a playlist

Pick **Convert to Playlist** in a session's options, on the **Sessions** page or in the player. Its current tracks go into a new playlist named after it. The session stays as it is, and tracks generated later aren't added.

### Search

The **Search** page looks across sessions, tracks and playlists, in titles, genres, musical traits, prompts and where a track came from. Type at least 2 characters.

- **Tabs:** **All**, **Sessions**, **Tracks** and **Playlists** each show an exact count.
- **Sort:** **Relevance**, **Newest first** or **Title A–Z**.
- **Search lyrics** searches only sung words, so only tracks can match, and instrumentals never do.
- **Matches:** each result names the field that matched and highlights your words.
- **Recent:** searches you acted on stay in your browser for next time.

Results are capped rather than paged: 5 per group under **All** and 50 on a single tab. If you hit the cap, narrow your search. If nothing matches, **Start a Session from** puts your words in the form on **Home** for you to review, and nothing is spent until you start it.

The **Sessions** page has its own search box for session names, track titles, genres and prompts, showing the best 50.

### Downloads

**Download Track** saves one MP3. **Download Session** and **Download Playlist** save a ZIP of MP3s, numbered in play order.

Files carry the title, session name, artist name, genres, year, tempo when known, cover, lyrics and how the track was made. They use the current names, so a renamed track downloads under its new name.

Only finished tracks can be downloaded, so a track reads "Still generating" until then. A ZIP leaves out tracks that aren't finished and tracks shared with you by someone else. What you may do with the files depends on your provider and plan. See [FAQ and license](/docs/faq-and-license).

### Session covers

Every session has one cover, shared by all its tracks. It starts on built-in placeholder art until its own cover lands. Choose in the **Cover art** fold on **Home**:

- **Use Tailored Cover Art Generation** asks your image provider for a cover in your chosen style. Turn it off to use the cover that comes with the music, at no additional cost.
- **Cover style** asks your image provider for **Auto**, **Random**, **Record Sleeve**, **Artistic**, **Photographic**, **Manga**, **Drawing**, **Minimal** or **Custom**. Styled covers need an image provider and cover budget. **Record Sleeve** paints an album cover from one decade between the 1960s and the 2000s: name a decade or an era's sound in your prompt ("80s synthwave", in any language), or it picks one for you.

To change it later, use the **Session Cover** tile in the player, or **Change Cover Art** in a session's options. You can set a **Cover style**, write up to 500 characters of **Art direction** or press **Use session prompt**. With a writing AI, a switch lets it polish your direction first. Press **Reroll Cover Art**, or **Upload your own**, which costs nothing.

A new cover is only a preview until you press **Save Cover Art**, so a cover you like is never replaced by accident. **Previous covers** keeps up to 10, uploads included. Delete one to make room for more. Changing the vibe or the name never changes the cover.

Rerolling needs an image provider, with styled covers turned on and budget left. Or ask [Roadie](#ask-roadie) for a new cover for a session or a playlist: "make a new cover for this, darker, neon city".

Everyone sets their own defaults in **Settings › Cover art**: **Use Tailored Cover Art Generation**, **Default cover style**, **Custom style definition** of up to 240 characters and **Enhance prompts**.

A good **Custom style definition** describes the medium, line, texture and contrast, not fixed colors or objects. Whatever you name shows up on every cover, so write "colors that fit the mood" instead of naming colors, and each session's theme picks the colors and subject. For example: *A risograph print look: two or three bold overlapping inks chosen to fit the mood, visible grain, slight misregistration and halftone dots, flat graphic shapes on uncoated paper.*

### Playlist covers

A playlist shows a mosaic of its tracks' covers until you set one. Open **Add Cover** or **Change Cover** in the card's menu, **Add Playlist Cover** or **Edit Playlist Cover** on its page, or the **Playlist Cover** tile in the player.

- **Upload image** sets your own picture straight away.
- **Generate** makes one from a **Cover style** and up to 500 characters of art direction. Press **Use this cover** to apply it.
- **Use People reference photos** draws the people named in the playlist's name or art direction, or you if nobody is named. It's off each time you open the sheet.
- **Previous covers** lets you go back, and **Use the mosaic** returns to the default.

### Rename anything

Pick **Rename Session** (up to 60 characters), **Rename Track** (up to 80) or **Rename Playlist** (up to 60). Your name shows across the app and in future downloads, and nothing overwrites it, but you can't get the generated name back.

Sessions rename from their options or the player, playlists from their menus or the player, and tracks only from the player's options menu. Renaming a track doesn't save it. Or ask [Roadie](#ask-roadie): "rename this track to Harbor Lights" or "call this session Sunday Coffee".

### Track data

**Track Data** shows how a track was made. Find it in the player's options menu or a playlist's track menu.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Title, album, artist, date generated, duration |
| **Prompts** | Your prompt, style prompt, expanded prompt |
| **Vibe** | Genres, activity, mood, energy, vocals, measured BPM |
| **Provenance** | Provider, model, license |

Fields without a value are hidden. The prompts show what they were when that track was made, even if the session has moved on.

### Removing and deleting

Each of these does something different. **Delete Track**, **Delete Playlist** and **Delete Session** ask first. **Reroll Track** acts at once, without asking.

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove from Playlist** | The track leaves this playlist. If it was its last one, it leaves your library but stays in **History** |
| **Remove from Library** | The track leaves Favorites and every playlist, and stays in **History** |
| **Reroll Track** | A dislike: the track goes and a new one comes in the same vibe |
| **Delete Track** | The audio is deleted for good, from every playlist and your library. Nothing is steered |
| **Delete Playlist** | The playlist goes. Tracks only in it leave your library and stay in **History** |
| **Delete Session** | The session stops and goes for good, with its unsaved tracks. Tracks in a playlist stay |

A deleted track still counts in your generated totals, and it lives on in [backups](/docs/backups) until they rotate out.

## Make it personal

### Learned taste

Liedmotif learns from what you play, favorite and keep in playlists. It uses that only for **For you** genre suggestions and Surprise Me, and never changes your normal sessions.

The controls are in **Settings › Playback › Personalization**. **Personalize suggestions** turns learning and its use off, while your stats keep recording. **Reset learned taste** starts again from what you do next. **Clear listening history** in **Your Stats** removes your listening records and your learned taste together.

### People

Add the people in your life, so a song or cover can really be about them. In **Settings › People** you have your own card plus up to five more. Each has a **Name**, an optional **About them** (**About you** on your own card) of up to 2,000 characters and up to three **Reference photos**.

To include someone else, use their saved name in your request. Capitals don't matter, but the word must match: "Mum" doesn't find someone saved as "Mother", and relationships or translations aren't worked out for you. Choose a distinctive name. You yourself don't need your name: see [You in your own songs](#you-in-your-own-songs).

- **Lyrics:** a named person can show up in the words of vocal tracks, and **About them** / **About you** reaches the lyrics only with a writing AI.
- **Session covers:** a named person with photos can appear on a styled cover. Basic covers can't include anyone.
- **Photos:** use JPEG, PNG or WebP. Two or three clear solo portraits work best, without group shots, sunglasses or heavy filters. They go only to your image provider, for covers.

Names and notes stay private on your instance unless a request needs them. Your own card can't be deleted.

### You in your own songs

Liedmotif is the soundtrack of your life, so you're part of your music without typing your name. This needs a text LLM.

- **Your details are in the lyrics of every vocal session.** **About you** shapes them, also when the song is about someone else: a birthday song for Clara is you singing to Clara.
- **You're in the scene when your request says so.** Then the lyrics name you next to the others, and you're on the cover with them. The text LLM goes by what you mean, not by particular words, in the language you write in. You don't need "me" or "I".

These examples have **Vocals** picked:

| Your request | Are you in the scene? | Lyrics | Cover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Me, Jonas, Linh and Clara on a trip to the moon" | Yes | You and the three of them, each with a part | You and the three of them |
| "With Jonas and Clara on the way back to Berlin" | Yes, "with" means you're there | You, Jonas and Clara | You, Jonas and Clara |
| "Auf dem Weg mit Jonas und Clara nach Berlin" | Yes | The same, in German | You, Jonas and Clara |
| "A birthday song for Clara" | No | You sing to Clara, with a touch of your own life | Clara |
| "Running through the waves at the beach" | No | From your point of view, with a touch of your own life | Your setting under [Your likeness on covers](#your-likeness-on-covers) decides |

Good to know:

- **It's the text LLM's call.** A wording that could mean either can go either way. To be sure, say "me" or use your saved name.
- **Instrumental sessions** have no lyrics, but a request that puts you in the scene still puts you on the cover.
- **The cover** needs a photo on your own card and a tailored cover, see [Session covers](#session-covers). **Never** under [Your likeness on covers](#your-likeness-on-covers) keeps you off.
- **Change Vibe** keeps the answer. You can change it there: "put me in it too" or "without me".
- **More Like This** and **Surprise Me** don't start from a request of yours, so nobody is read into the scene. Their cover follows your setting under [Your likeness on covers](#your-likeness-on-covers).
- **Without a text LLM,** nothing is read into your request. Only saved names count, and About text doesn't reach the lyrics.
- **To leave yourself out** of one session, turn on [Run without my profile details](#run-without-my-profile-details).

### Your likeness on covers

Under **When nobody is named in the music prompt**, **Include the owner in Covers** sets how often your own photo may appear: **Always**, **Regularly**, **Seldom** or **Never**. It needs a photo on your own card, and it saves as soon as you pick. A request that includes you counts as naming you, so your photo is used whatever you picked here, except **Never**.

**Never** keeps every personal photo on your instance, so no cover uses a photo of anyone, named or not. It doesn't stop a named person's notes from reaching the lyrics.

Until your own card has a photo, the setting is locked and shows **Never**, but that isn't the opt-out yet: photos of people you name can still go to your image provider. To block them, add a photo to your own card and pick **Never**.

### Run without my profile details

Turn on **Run without my profile details** on **Home** to keep your own details out of one session's lyrics and cover: your profile, your **About you** and your photos. That holds even if you type your own name or say "me". People you name in the request are still used, so "a birthday song for Clara" is then about Clara alone. It's off again on your next visit. A later Change Vibe respects it, and More Like This starts with personalization back on.

### Profile and artist name

**Settings › Profile** holds your photo, **First name**, **Email**, **City**, **Country**, **Artist name** and **Short description**.

- **City** and **Country** give your sessions a sense of place.
- **Artist name** is written into downloaded MP3s, up to 80 characters. Without one, your name is used.
- **Short description** appears on your lounge profile card, up to 80 characters. It's never used for music.
- **Change password** is here too.

Only the admin can change their sign-in email. A member's is fixed.

## Share with your people

The admin can invite family and friends to the same instance. Each member gets their own private sessions, library and settings, on the admin's provider keys. In the lounge, you can share any playlist except Favorites with everyone or with selected people, and listeners hear it live as you keep curating. Nothing leaves your instance. See [members and the lounge](/docs/members).

## Your stats

Open **Your Stats** from the menu behind your avatar, and pick **7 days**, **30 days** or **All time**.

- **Listening** shows your listening time, plays, tracks and a chart per day.
- **Most played** ranks your tracks. Tap one to play it in its session.
- **Most listened genres** ranks genres by listening time.
- **Generation** shows how many tracks were generated, split into heard and never heard, plus failed attempts.

A play counts after 30 seconds, or half of a track shorter than a minute. Listening time counts every second the app is playing, so not while it's paused, buffering or silenced in the app. Stats cover your own tracks only, and generated tracks stay counted after you delete them.

**Clear listening history** under **History** removes your listening records and learned taste for good, and it asks first. Your music, playlists, generated totals and costs stay.

The admin also gets a **Costs** tab comparing estimated costs. See [costs and limits](/docs/costs-and-limits).

## Stay in control of cost

You pay your providers directly, and Liedmotif adds no subscription. Limits read in tracks. When a budget runs out, a running session repeats its own tracks at no cost, and it picks up fresh music when the budget renews. The whole picture is in [costs and limits](/docs/costs-and-limits).

## Use it on your phone, tablet and computer

### One web app

Liedmotif is a web app, so it runs in the browser on your phones, tablets and computers. On a narrow screen it has a bottom navigation bar, and on a wide one a sidebar you can collapse. Your instance has to be reachable from the device first. See [phone access](/docs/install/phone-access).

### Add it to your home screen

Add Liedmotif to your phone's home screen to run it like an app, in its own window. It's made for portrait, so the installed app asks you to turn your phone upright in landscape. [Phone access](/docs/install/phone-access#add-it-to-your-home-screen) shows how.

### Light and Night

Pick **Mode** in the menu behind your avatar to switch between **Light** and **Night**. Your browser remembers the choice.

### Reduced motion

Liedmotif respects your device's reduced-motion setting and tones down movement that isn't needed.
